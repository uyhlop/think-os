How this demo works

This is a front-end proof-of-concept. A student fills out the form, the system checks the required details, and then the user sees a clear confirmation that the application was received.

1. Student applies They enter contact details, school info, and what they struggle with while preparing for nursing exams.
2. Form checks the basics Required fields, email format, and short written answers are validated before submission.
3. Confirmation appears The student sees a friendly success screen that confirms next steps and expected follow-up.
