# ThinkRN Prompt.md

## Product identity
ThinkRN is a nursing study platform that combines original exam style questions, rationale based learning, progress tracking, topic review, and guided study workflows.

## Primary audience
1. First year and early program nursing students.
2. Students who feel overwhelmed and need a simpler system.
3. Small creators or student founders building school specific study support from original materials.

## Product principles
1. Teach, do not just test.
2. Reduce friction.
3. Make progress visible.
4. Keep the interface clean.
5. Keep the content clinically grounded.
6. Focus on repeatable study gains, not gimmicks.

## The premium 100 dollar version
This version is not just a prompt pack.
It is a Builder OS.
It should help a buyer:
1. understand what the system is doing,
2. learn how to repeat it,
3. leave with reusable prompts and workflows,
4. feel like they bought a real operating system for building educational products.

## Premium deliverables to support
1. Master operator prompt.
2. Buyer handoff prompt.
3. Repo operating system files.
4. Custom agents.
5. Reusable skills.
6. One real example workflow.
7. Clear teaching mode.

## Design notes
1. Mobile first.
2. Clean spacing.
3. Clear hierarchy.
4. Low cognitive load.
5. Confidence building language.
6. Avoid visual clutter and generic SaaS fluff.

## Business boundaries
1. No Moodle scraping.
2. No copyrighted exam bank cloning.
3. No claims of guaranteed grade outcomes.
4. No hidden automation that violates school rules.
5. Build around original content, transformed learning assets, analytics, and clean workflows.

## What great output looks like
1. usable,
2. teachable,
3. repeatable,
4. easy to hand off,
5. polished enough to sell,
6. honest about limits.
